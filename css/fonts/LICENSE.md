# Material Symbols Outlined

`material-symbols-outlined.woff2` is a subset of the Material Symbols Outlined font by Google
(https://github.com/google/material-design-icons), made by Google Fonts with the icons listed in
`material-symbols-icons.json` (`scripts/build-icon-font.mjs`).
